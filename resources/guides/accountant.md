# Accountant Guide

This guide is for the school accountant. Each topic starts with a short note on how it is designed to work, then the steps. Menu names can differ slightly between schools; the steps name the screen to look for.

## How the money model works

**How it works.** The system uses **cash basis** accounting: money counts when it is received or paid, not when it is billed.

- **Income** is fee payments received (the amount paid plus any fine paid) plus any income you record directly, such as donations or rent.
- **Expenses** are the expense entries you record. Voided and pending entries are not counted.
- **Accounts** are the places where money sits: Cash, Bank and Mobile Money. Every payment, expense and transfer moves an account balance.
- **Categories** label income and expenses so reports and budgets can group them.

School fees are collected through the **Fees** screens, not entered as ordinary income. They flow into income totals automatically, so the income category list holds items like donations or rent, not tuition.

## Your dashboard

**How it works.** The dashboard opens with **Finance at a glance**, so you see money first.

- Money tiles for income, expenses, net and fees collected.
- A pending banner when entries wait for approval.
- An income vs expenses chart for 6 or 12 months.
- Expenses by category, fee collection progress and account balances.
- A **Budget watch** card once budgets are set.
- Recent transactions with a link to the cashbook.

## Accounts and opening balances

**How it works.** Each account starts with an opening balance on a go-live date. After that, the balance is the opening balance plus money in, minus money out. Set opening balances once, before recording anything else.

1. Open **Finance**, then **Accounts**.
2. Add Cash, Bank and Mobile Money accounts.
3. Enter each opening balance and the go-live date.
4. To stop using an account, switch it off rather than deleting one that has history.

## Finance categories

**How it works.** Categories are separate for income and expenses. Budgets and the expense chart use them, so keep names consistent and few.

1. Open **Finance**, then **Categories**.
2. Add income categories (for example Donations, Rent) and expense categories (for example Salaries, Utilities, Repairs and Maintenance).
3. Switch off a category that is no longer needed instead of deleting it.

## Fee structures

**How it works.** A fee structure says which fee items each class pays in a year or term, and how much. A student's balance is the structure, minus any discount, minus payments.

1. Open **Fees**, then **Structures**.
2. Choose the academic year and class.
3. Add the fee items (tuition, PTA, books) with amounts.
4. Save, then open one student's fee statement to confirm the total.

## Discounts and scholarships

**How it works.** A discount is a named reduction that you assign to individual students. The fee position then counts the reduced amount.

1. Open **Fees**, then **Discounts**, and create the discount.
2. Assign it to the student. Use unassign to remove it.
3. Check the student's statement to confirm the new balance.

## Collecting fees and issuing receipts

**How it works.** A payment is recorded against the student, an account and a date. The system allocates it across the student's fee items and numbers the receipt in sequence (RCP-000001, RCP-000002 and so on). Receipts can be viewed or downloaded as PDF.

1. Open **Fees**, then **Collect**.
2. Find the student by name or admission number.
3. Enter the amount, payment method, the account that receives it and the date.
4. Confirm. The receipt appears at once.
5. Download the PDF or print it for the payer.

> **Tip:** to correct a mistake, void the payment instead of editing it, then record it again.

## Income, expenses and transfers

**How it works.** Other money movements are entered as transactions with a date, category, account, amount and note. A **transfer** moves money between your own accounts (for example Cash to Bank) and does not change income or expenses.

1. Open **Finance** and choose income, expense or transfer.
2. Fill in the date, category, account, amount and a short description.
3. Save. If approvals are on, the entry stays pending until an administrator approves it.
4. To undo an entry, open it and choose **Void**. It stays visible but no longer counts.

## Approvals

**How it works.** Pending entries do not count toward totals until approved. The dashboard banner shows how many are waiting.

1. Open the approvals list from the dashboard banner or the Finance menu.
2. Review each entry, then approve or reject it.

## The cashbook

**How it works.** The cashbook is a chronological ledger of everything that moved in one account, with a running balance. It is the quickest way to reconcile against the bank statement or cash in hand.

1. Open **Finance**, then **Cashbook**.
2. Choose the account and date range.
3. Compare the closing balance with the real balance and investigate any difference.

## Budgets and budget watch

**How it works.** A budget is an amount per expense category for an academic year. Spending counts all active expenses in that category between the year's start and end dates. The dashboard **Budget watch** card ranks categories by the share of budget used. A category shows **Near limit** from 80% and **Over budget** from 100%.

1. Open **Finance**, then **Budget**.
2. Choose the academic year.
3. Enter an amount for each expense category you want to control, then save.
4. Check the dashboard. Categories without a budget are not tracked.

## Reports

**How it works.** Reports are read-only summaries computed from the same entries, so they always agree with the dashboard.

- **Income and expenditure** statement for a date range.
- **Balance report** of account balances.
- **Student fee statement** with billed, discounted, paid and balance.

1. Open **Finance** and choose the report.
2. Set the dates, then view or print.

## Go-live checklist

Run through this before real money is recorded.

- [ ] Remove all test payments and entries.
- [ ] Reset the receipt counter so the first real receipt is RCP-000001.
- [ ] Create Cash, Bank and Mobile Money accounts with real opening balances and the go-live date.
- [ ] Set up income and expense categories.
- [ ] Check every fee structure against the approved fee schedule.
- [ ] Recreate real discounts and assign them.
- [ ] Record one test payment, check the receipt and the dashboard, then void it.

## Common problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A student's balance looks wrong | Open the fee statement and check the structure, discounts and any voided payment |
| Dashboard and report totals differ | Check the date range, and whether entries are pending or voided |
| An account balance does not match the bank | Use the cashbook for that account and look for missing or voided entries |
| A category is missing from the budget page | Make sure it is an active expense category |
| A page shows a server error | Note what you were doing and tell the system administrator |
