# Teacher Guide

This guide is for teachers. Each topic starts with a short note on how it is designed to work, then the steps. Menu names can differ slightly between schools; the steps name the screen to look for.

## Signing in and your dashboard

**How it works.** A teacher account shows only the classes and subjects the administrator has assigned to you. If a class or subject is missing, ask the administrator to assign it.

1. Open the school address and choose the staff login.
2. Enter your email and password. If you forget it, use **Forgot password**.
3. Your dashboard shows your classes and what needs attention.

## My classes and students

1. Open **My Classes** to see each class you teach.
2. Choose a class to see its students.
3. Open **My Students** for one combined list across your classes.

## Taking attendance

**How it works.** Attendance is a daily register per class: one mark per student per day. You can correct a day after saving it.

1. Open **Attendance** and choose the class and date.
2. Mark each student present, absent or late.
3. Save. Open the same date again to correct a mistake.

## Entering marks

**How it works.** You enter the raw mark out of the exam's maximum for your subject. The system works out the percentage, grade and remark for you, so you never type grades.

1. Open **Exams** and choose the exam, class and subject.
2. Type each student's mark. Leave a student blank if they were absent.
3. Save. Grades and remarks fill in automatically.
4. Open **Results** to check the class and spot missing marks.
5. Add your end-of-term comment for each student where asked.

> **Tip:** a mark above the maximum is rejected. Re-enter it correctly.

## Homework

**How it works.** Homework you post appears in the student portal for that class. You can see who has submitted.

1. Open **Homework** and choose to add new homework.
2. Select the class and subject, add instructions and a due date, and save.
3. Open the homework later to see submissions.

## Lesson plans

1. Open **Lesson Plans** and choose to create a new one.
2. Pick the class and subject, then write the objectives, activities and resources.
3. Save. You can edit or delete it later.

## Timetable

1. Open **Timetable** to see your weekly periods.
2. If something is wrong, ask the administrator to correct it.

## Promotion

**How it works.** At year end the administrator promotes students to the next class. If your school lets class teachers propose promotions, you can review your class and submit it for confirmation.

1. Open **Promotion**.
2. Review the students in your class and untick anyone who should repeat.
3. Submit. The administrator confirms.

## My profile

1. Open **My Profile**.
2. Update your phone number, photo and password, then save.

## Common problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| I cannot see a class | Ask the administrator to assign you to the class and subject |
| A student's grade shows a dash | Tell the administrator; the grade scale may not cover that percentage |
| I cannot save a mark | Check it is a number and not above the maximum |
| I forgot my password | Use **Forgot password** on the login page |
