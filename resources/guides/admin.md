# Administrator Guide

This guide is for school administrators. Each topic starts with a short note on how it is designed to work, then the steps. Menu names can differ slightly between schools; the steps name the screen to look for.

## Signing in and the dashboard

**How it works.** Staff sign in with an email and password. After sign-in you land on the dashboard, which is built for your role. Administrators see a **Finance at a glance** block first, then school statistics.

1. Open the school address and choose the staff login.
2. Enter your email and password. If you forget it, use **Forgot password** and follow the emailed link.
3. Update your name, photo and password from **My profile**.

The dashboard shows money tiles, a pending-approvals banner, an income vs expenses chart, expenses by category, fee collection, account balances, a budget watch card (once budgets exist) and school counts.

## First-time setup order

**How it works.** Everything hangs off the **academic year**. Classes, students, fees, exams, marks and attendance are all recorded against the current year, so set it up first.

1. School settings: name, logo, colours, contact details.
2. Academic year and terms; mark one year as current.
3. Classes and sections, then subjects, then attach subjects to classes.
4. Staff accounts, then assign teachers to classes and subjects.
5. Grade scales.
6. Students (add or import), then student PINs.
7. Finance: accounts, categories, fee structures, discounts (see the Accountant Guide).
8. Timetable, attendance and exams as the term starts.

## School settings and branding

**How it works.** One settings page holds the school name, motto, logo, contact details, mail settings and colours. The sidebar, buttons, emails and printed pages all use the school colour, so changing it once restyles the system.

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Enter the school name, motto, address, phone and email.
3. Upload the logo. It also becomes the browser tab icon.
4. Pick a colour theme, or set the sidebar and accent colours yourself.
5. Add mail settings if you want report cards and PINs emailed to parents.
6. Save, then refresh the page to see the new colours.

## Academic years and terms

**How it works.** Exactly one academic year is **current**. New fees, exams and attendance attach to it, and the dashboard warns you when the year is about to end. Terms sit inside the year.

1. Open **Academic years**.
2. Add the year with its start and end dates and its terms.
3. Choose **Set current** on the year now in session.

> **Tip:** do not delete a year that already has marks or payments. Make another year current instead.

## Classes, sections and subjects

**How it works.** A class (for example JHS 1) can have several sections (A, B). Subjects are created once, then attached to the classes that study them. The class is the unit used for the timetable, attendance, fees and promotion.

1. Open **Classes** and add each class.
2. Open a class and add its sections.
3. Open **Subjects** and add each subject.
4. On the class page, attach the subjects it studies.

## Staff and teachers

**How it works.** A staff record is both the person's profile and their sign-in. Teachers are assigned to classes and subjects so they only see their own classes. Documents such as contracts can be attached to the record.

1. Open **Staff** and choose **Add staff**.
2. Fill in personal details, role (teacher, accountant, admin and so on) and email.
3. Save. The person signs in with that email and can reset their password.
4. Assign teachers to classes and subjects on the class page.
5. Use the record's **Documents** area to upload files.

## Students

**How it works.** Each student has a profile, a class and section, guardian details and documents. A student can be **withdrawn** and later **reinstated**; their history is kept.

**Add one student**

1. Open **Students** and choose **Add student**.
2. Fill in names, date of birth, class, section and guardian contacts.
3. Save. The student appears in the class list.

**Import many students**

1. Open **Students**, then **Import**.
2. Download the template, fill it in and keep the column headings unchanged.
3. Upload the file and check the preview.
4. Confirm the import and review any rows reported as errors.

**Withdraw or reinstate**

1. Open the student's profile.
2. Choose **Withdraw**, give a reason and confirm. Use **Reinstate** to bring the student back.

## Student PINs

**How it works.** Students and parents sign in on the student page with an admission number and a PIN issued by the school. The student changes the PIN at first login.

1. Open **Students**, then **PINs**.
2. Generate PINs for a class or selected students.
3. Print the list or email the PINs to guardians.
4. To reset a forgotten PIN, generate a new one for that student.

## Timetables and attendance

**How it works.** The class timetable is built from periods and subject assignments. Teachers mark attendance per class per day; administrators can view and correct it. A separate exam timetable lists paper dates.

1. Open **Timetable**, choose the class and add periods with subject and teacher.
2. Use the view option for a printable timetable.
3. Open **Attendance** to see daily registers by class and date.
4. Open the exam timetable to schedule papers for an exam.

## Grade scales

**How it works.** A grade scale maps a percentage to a grade and remark (for example 80 to 100 is A1, Excellent). Grades are assigned automatically when marks are saved, using the scale of the exam's own academic year.

1. Open **Grade scales**.
2. Add each band with its minimum, maximum, grade and remark.
3. Make sure the bands cover 0 to 100 with no gaps or overlaps.

## Exams, marks and report cards

**How it works.** An exam belongs to a term. You add a schedule (subject, class, maximum marks, date). Teachers enter marks per subject, and the system turns each mark into a percentage, grade and remark. Report cards combine all subjects with positions, a term comparison and comments.

1. Open **Exams** and create the exam for the term.
2. Add the schedule: subject, class and maximum marks.
3. Teachers enter marks, or you enter them from the mark-entry screen.
4. Open **Results** to see results by class and check for blank entries.
5. Save the term report comments for each student.
6. Open a student's **Report card** to preview it, download the PDF, or email it to the guardian.

> **Tip:** if a grade shows as a dash, the mark has no grade yet. Check that the grade scale covers that percentage.

## Homework, lesson plans and notices

**How it works.** Teachers post homework and lesson plans, and students see homework in their portal. Notices go on the school noticeboard.

1. Open **Notices**, add a title and message, and choose who sees it.
2. Open **Homework** or **Lesson plans** to review what teachers have posted.

## Admissions

**How it works.** Applications submitted from the public website arrive in the admissions list with a status, so you can review them before creating a student record.

1. Open **Admissions**.
2. Open an application and update its status.
3. For accepted applicants, add them under **Students**.

## Promotion

**How it works.** At year end, promotion moves students to the next class. Run it after results are final and the new academic year exists.

1. Create the new academic year first.
2. Open **Promotion** and choose the class to promote from and to.
3. Untick any student who should repeat, then confirm.

## Website and gallery

**How it works.** The public website reads its content from the system: hero images, programs, news and gallery. Changes show on the site straight away.

1. Open **Website**.
2. Upload or remove hero images.
3. Add news stories with a title, text and image.
4. Manage programs and gallery images in their own tabs.

## Finance overview

**How it works.** Administrators have the same finance screens as the accountant: accounts, fee structures, discounts, collecting fees, expenses, budgets and reports. Day-to-day finance work is normally done by the accountant; see the Accountant Guide for the full steps. Use the dashboard for a quick view of income, expenses, balances and fee collection.

## Activity log

**How it works.** Important actions (sign-ins, edits, voids) are recorded with who did them and when. The activity log is visible to the super administrator.

1. Open **Activity Log** to see recent actions and filter by user or date.
2. To stop someone's access, deactivate their account rather than deleting it.

## Common problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A student's grade shows a dash | Check the grade scale covers that percentage and the mark was saved |
| A teacher cannot see a class | Assign the teacher to the class and subject |
| A student cannot sign in | Issue a new PIN and check the admission number |
| A report card email did not arrive | Check the guardian's email, the mail settings and the spam folder |
| Colours or logo did not change | Refresh the page, or clear the browser cache |
| A page shows a server error | Note what you were doing and tell the system administrator |
