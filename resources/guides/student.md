# Student and Parent Guide

This guide is for students and parents. Each topic starts with a short note on how it is designed to work, then the steps.

## Signing in and your PIN

**How it works.** The school gives each student an admission number and a PIN. The portal shows only that student's information, and the only thing you can change is the PIN.

1. Open the school's student login page.
2. Enter the admission number and PIN given by the school.
3. At first login you will be asked to choose a new PIN. Pick one you will remember and keep it private.
4. To change it later, open **Change PIN**.
5. If you forget it, ask the school office for a new one.

## Your dashboard

**How it works.** The dashboard is a summary of your class: recent results, notices and homework.

1. After signing in, read the cards on the dashboard.
2. Use the menu on the side to open any topic below.

## Results and report card

**How it works.** Results appear after the teacher has entered marks. For each subject you see the mark, percentage, grade and remark. The report card adds your class position and a comparison with earlier terms.

1. Open **Results** and choose the exam or term.
2. Read each subject's grade. Ask your class teacher if something looks wrong.
3. Open the report card for the exam and choose to download the PDF.
4. The school may also email the report card to the parent's address. Check the spam folder if it does not arrive.

## Fees

**How it works.** The fees page shows what has been billed, any discount, what has been paid and what is still owing. Payments appear after the school's accountant records them.

1. Open **Fees**.
2. Check the amount owing and the payments received.
3. If a payment is missing, take your receipt to the accounts office.

## Attendance

1. Open **Attendance**.
2. Check the days marked present, absent or late.
3. Talk to your class teacher if a day is wrong.

## Homework

1. Open **Homework** to see tasks for your class and their due dates.
2. Open an item to read the instructions.

## Notices

1. Open **Notices** to read announcements from the school.

## Timetable and exam timetable

1. Open **Timetable** to see your weekly lessons.
2. Open **Exam Timetable** to see the dates of your papers.

## Exams Practice

**How it works.** Exams Practice is a separate practice site for exam questions. It opens in a new tab.

1. Choose **Exams Practice** in the menu.
2. Follow the instructions on the new page.

## My profile

1. Open **Profile** to check your details.
2. Tell the school office if anything is wrong.

## Common problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| I cannot sign in | Check the admission number, then ask the school office for a new PIN |
| My results are missing | The teacher may not have entered them yet; ask your class teacher |
| The report card email did not arrive | Check the spam folder, then ask the school to confirm the email address |
| My fee balance looks wrong | Take your receipts to the accounts office |
